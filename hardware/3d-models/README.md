# 3D Models — Chassis

The robot's chassis was designed in-house in Fusion 360 and 3D-printed.

- **STL file:** [chassis.stl](chassis.stl) — ready to slice and print
- **Fusion 360 source (editable):** [View on Fusion 360](https://student395829.autodesk360.com/g/shares/SH28cd1QT2badd0ea72bd2512c9273c0f380)

If you're replicating this build, open the STL in your slicer of choice (Cura, PrusaSlicer, etc.). See `media/chassis.jpg` and `media/fusion-design.png` for photos of the printed and modeled chassis.
